04Warped ΔMRT visualiser

Relief shows ΔMRT with the tree minus without it: blue valleys indicate cooling, white is near zero and red peaks indicate warming. Layers are terrain-flattened; relief encodes temperature, and the pedestrian, trunk and branches are schematic references.
